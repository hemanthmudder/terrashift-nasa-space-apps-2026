Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data

Turn NASA Earth Observations into Smarter Farming Decisions.

Explore climate-aware crop rotations using NASA environmental data, local soil information and agricultural knowledge.

1. Genuine NASA Earth Observations

Retrieves location-specific daily agroclimatology parameters from NASA POWER (MERRA-2 / CERES): mean/max/min temperature (T2M, T2M_MAX, T2M_MIN), corrected precipitation (PRECTOTCORR), relative humidity (RH2M), solar radiation (ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN), and surface vs. root-zone soil wetness (GWETTOP, GWETROOT), alongside GPM IMERG, SMAP, and MODIS spatial resolution disclosures.

2. Verified Karnataka Crop Database

Evaluates 12 crops across Karnataka's Agro-Climatic Zones (Ragi, Maize, Paddy, Sorghum, Pigeon Pea, Chickpea, Green Gram, Black Gram, Groundnut, Sunflower, Sesame, and Sunnhemp/Dhaincha Green Manure) with citations from ICAR, UAS Bangalore, ICRISAT, and TNAU.

3. Explainable Two-Stage Rotation Engine

Stage A screens crop suitability across climate, rainfall, water, and soil pH without disqualifying farms with missing soil tests. Stage B builds Balanced, Water-Conscious, and Soil-Improvement sequences with explicit monoculture and family-repetition penalties.
